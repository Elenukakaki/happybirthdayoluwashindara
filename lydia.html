LYDIA

A SPECIAL BIRTHDAY STORY

Happy Birthday Lydia

Happy Birthday, Lydia 🎉

I genuinely wish you long life and prosperity…
but that feels like what everyone says.

So let me say it differently.

I wish you peace, joy, growth,
and a beautiful life ahead ❤️✨

Nothing extra —
just a good life like everyone hopes for.

But beyond that…

The fact that you are alive today is already a gift.
And I believe it's because God truly loves you.

"You are an amazing person"
— and I don't say that lightly.

I've met many people… but you?

You broke the definition of "special."

And don't worry…
I still rank myself number one 😌
Just kidding.

You are special in every real way.

And this is only the beginning…

First Day We Spoke

Let's talk about the first day we spoke.

It was my birthday…
and I honestly was not happy that day.

Not because of everything around me —
but because a lot of people I expected to remember didn't.

Strangely though,
I still got wishes from people I never expected.

And somehow, those unexpected people made my day better.

You were one of them.

I remember telling you later
that you were like the "topping on ice cream."

Because ice cream on its own is fine…
but the toppings are what make it special.

That was exactly what you did.

You didn't just add to the day…
You changed how it felt.

There's a calm about you
that makes everything feel lighter.

First Time We Met

Now to the day we met for the first time.

I was nervous… really nervous.

My heart was beating fast, and I kept praying:
"God, please don't let me mess this up."

I prayed more that day than usual…
just because I was about to see you.

And then I finally did.

Yes, I was late —
and I'll still apologise for that.
Even though you also tried to take the blame.
But I'm still sorry.

We talked, we laughed, we shared moments…

But inside me, I was overwhelmed.

Because sitting in front of you felt unreal.

Chills. Literal chills.

You have this presence
that doesn't ask for attention…
It naturally takes it.

It felt like the whole room changed when you walked in.
Like everything became lighter, brighter, alive.

And I know that's not just that day.
That's you.

Your Energy

Then we started talking.

And somehow, we never ran out of things to say.

Food, culture, work, politics… even football.

And yes — I'm still very happy
you're a Manchester United fan 😌❤️

Don't worry… I'll still take you to a ball house one day
so we can watch men lose their minds together.

But beyond the jokes…

Our conversations became something deeper.

You speak honestly, clearly, and without hesitation.
You say what you mean… and you mean what you say.

No confusion. No pretending.

And I respect that a lot.

We spoke about your ideologies,
your culture, your mindset…

And even though it hasn't been long —
not even up to a month yet —
I've already learned a lot from you.
More than I expected.

And one more thing…

You listen. Properly.

Not waiting for your turn to speak —
you actually listen.

Even when you're tired, you still stay.
You still talk.

And hearing your voice became something
I genuinely looked forward to.

Final Wish

Finally…

I don't want to say too much.

I just want to wish you a happy birthday, Lydia.

I pray you enjoy good health,
peace of mind, and real happiness.

And yes… I pray for wealth too 😌
Because you give that "rich kid energy" naturally.

I pray this new year of your life
brings only good things.

No sorrow. No pain.

You will enjoy every day of it.

God will never leave you, nor forsake you.

When you look back,
you will see peace…
and growth… and beautiful memories.

Happy Birthday, Lydia.

God bless you ❤️

To Lydia 💖

🎂 A STORY THAT DOESN'T END HERE 🎂
